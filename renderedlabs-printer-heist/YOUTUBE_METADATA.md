## 2. Description

In February 2016, hackers executed the most audacious cyber heist in financial history, infiltrating Bangladesh Bank’s SWIFT terminal in Dhaka to siphon nearly $1,000,000,000. 

To cover their tracks, custom malware intercepted and deleted the bank’s HP LaserJet printer queue—leaving the output tray completely empty. Staff arrived on Friday morning assuming it was an ordinary paper jam. By the time the printer was rebooted on Saturday and spit out dozens of frantic inquiries, $81 million had already vanished through Manila casinos. 

The only thing that stopped the rest of the billion dollars? A single typo in a $20 million wire order that alerted an eagle-eyed routing clerk.
