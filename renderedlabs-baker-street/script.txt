# The 1971 Baker Street Bank Heist — Sealed Until 2071
In nineteen seventy-one, a twenty-ton vault door seemed unbreachable.
So a gang bypassed it through the floor.
From a cellar, they dropped five feet, tunneled forty feet under a restaurant, and hauled eight tons of dirt.
Fifteen feet up, three feet of reinforced concrete stopped them.
Their jack sank into a well, and their thermal lance choked in fumes.
So gelignite blasted a twelve-inch hole through the floor.
Two hundred and sixty-eight boxes emptied. One point five million stolen.
A radio ham intercepted their walkie-talkie:
[Operator] Money may be your God, but it's not mine.
Police checked seven hundred banks, but only checked the doors.
Britain sealed the files until twenty seventy-one.
