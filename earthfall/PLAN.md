## Narration (34 lines)

1. This is the deepest hole humans have ever dug.
2. Twelve kilometres. That's it.
3. The Earth is six thousand, three hundred and seventy-one kilometres from surface to center.
4. We've explored two tenths of one percent of the way down.
5. So here's a thought experiment: drill a tunnel straight through the planet.
6. Line it with something that can't melt. Pump out the air.
7. Then step in.
8. You fall. After ten seconds, you're moving a hundred metres per second.
9. Gravity is strongest at the surface.
10. In a uniform planet, it weakens in a straight line: half as strong at the halfway point.
11. At the center, it cancels out completely.
12. For one perfect moment, you float.
13. Then you're past it, and gravity pulls you back.
14. You slow down, and stop at the far end.
15. The trip takes forty-two minutes.
16. That's the clean version. The real Earth is denser, so the fall is closer to thirty-eight minutes.
17. At the center, you'd be moving eight kilometres per second.
18. But the center is not a cave.
19. It's iron, almost as hot as the Sun's surface: five thousand four hundred degrees.
20. The pressure is three point six million atmospheres.
21. Rock flows like putty. Metal is squeezed solid.
22. Your tunnel would sag shut behind you.
23. The deepest hole we've made is twelve kilometres deep.
24. At the bottom, the rock is one hundred and eighty degrees. It creeps back into the hole.
25. The drill bit gets stuck. The hole heals.
26. Unless your tunnel runs pole to pole, the Earth turns under you.
27. You drift sideways, and scrape the wall.
28. With air, you'd hit terminal velocity: two hundred kilometres per hour.
29. You'd never reach the other side.
30. So a tunnel through the Earth is impossible.
31. But the maths is beautiful.
32. A hole through the center is a shortcut to the other side.
33. And most of the time, that other side is ocean.
34. The deepest we've managed is a pinprick.

## Facts and sources (checked)

- Kola Superdeep Borehole: 12,262 m; 23 cm diameter; bottom ~180 °C; rock became plastic and crept into the hole;
  drilling stopped in 1992. — USGS Open-File Report 86-517; NASA; Wikipedia summary.
- Earth mean radius 6,371 km; 12.262 / 6,371 = 0.19%. — IUGG / NASA.
- Gravity tunnel: 42 min 12 s for a uniform Earth; 38 min 11 s with PREM realistic density; peak speed ~8 km/s. —
  Klotz 2013 (arXiv:1308.1342); AAAS Science news; gravity-train literature.
- Inner core: ~5,400 °C (5,100–5,700 °C), pressure ~330–360 GPa (≈3.3–3.6 million atm). — Alfè et al.; PREM; USGS.
- Coriolis deflection for a non-polar tunnel; pole-to-pole tunnel avoids it. — geophysics literature; Alaska
  Geophysical Institute; Ask a Mathematician.
- Terminal velocity of a belly-down human ~55 m/s ≈ 200 km/h. — standard skydiving references.
- Antipodal land is rare: only ~4% of Earth's surface is land opposite land; most land faces ocean. — Wikipedia
  "Antipodes on Earth"; NOAA.
